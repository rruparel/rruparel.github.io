One phrase, five meanings

What does “poorly controlled diabetes” mean?

Before reading, each person answered this question. Their answers came from emergency rooms, exam rooms, workplaces and their own walking shoes.

P4 changes her answer

Before
“So to me, poorly controlled means, you didn’t do your part.”
“I did it, so I know it can be done.”
On the page
Causes: not enough medication, too much food, not enough physical activity, and stress or illness.
Reading
“Tension can make your sugar high? I didn’t know that.”
“I thought it was because I was eating badly. Maybe it was also the tension.”
After
poorly controlled means you didn’t do your part.“Now I think, maybe sometimes you did your part and still it went up.”
Her friend
“I will not tell her, ‘Just walk more.’ I used to say that.”
